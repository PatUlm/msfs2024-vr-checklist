# AS350 B3e facts used by the H125 checklist

Sources and document versions are identified in the adjacent
[README](README.md). These are selected technical facts used in the simulator
checklist, not a reproduction of the manual's full procedure or an approved
flight document.

| App topic | Technical fact used | Printed source page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prestart | Rotor brake released; twist grip at idle; lighting selected for day/night conditions. | 4-6–4-7, section 4.3.1 |
| Generator | Engine N1 at least 67% before generator activation. | 4-8, section 4.3.2 |
| Start configuration | Pitot heat, fuel-pump shutdown, starter guard and avionics are covered by the engine-start procedure. | 4-8, section 4.3.2 |
| Horn | Activation at rotor speed of at least 340 RPM. | 4-9–4-10, section 4.3.3 |
| Shutdown cooling | 30 seconds at idle before engine shutdown. | 4-14, section 4.8 |
| Rotor brake | Normal application at no more than 140 rotor RPM. | 4-14, section 4.8 |
| Anti-collision light | Shutdown after the rotor stops. | 4-14, section 4.8 |
| Exterior lighting | DAY/NIGHT selection couples position lights to cockpit lighting; anti-collision lighting has a separate control. | Complementary Flight Manual 7.6, chapter 15-16, pp. 1–3; supplementary source caveat in README |

The canonical app sequence remains in
[airbus-h125.json](../../data/airbus-h125.json). Original documents are linked
from the adjacent README.
