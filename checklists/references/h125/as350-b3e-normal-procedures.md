# AS350 B3e normal procedures relevant to the checklist

This is a normalized, narrowly scoped extraction of checklist-relevant data
from the flight-manual excerpt identified in the adjacent
[`README.md`](README.md). Control names, target states, thresholds, and order
are retained; typography and prose are condensed. It is research evidence, not
an approved operational checklist.

## 4.3.3 Run-up Check

Source pages: 4-9 and 4-10. The detailed hydraulic accumulator and pressure
isolation tests precede the following sequence.

| Order | Control or check | Required state or action |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| — | Engine oil temperature | Wait until the required minimum is reached |
| 2 | Twist grip | Flight position |
| 3 | Horn | On when rotor speed is at least 340 RPM |
| — | Horn audio warning | Check on at or below 360 RPM and off above 360 RPM |
| 4 | Rotor-speed indication | Check in the lower green range |
| 5 | Fire test | Perform; check fire indication and gong |
| 6 | Parameters | Check warnings, electrical voltage/current, and engine oil pressure |

## Exterior-lighting relationship

Supplementary-system source: Complementary Flight Manual 7.6, chapter 15-16,
pages 1 through 3; see the provenance note in [`README.md`](README.md).

The cockpit-lighting selector is `OFF/DAY/NIGHT` (or `OFF/DAY/NIGHT/NVG` when
installed). In a standard installation, selecting Day or Night activates the
integrated cockpit lighting and automatically illuminates the position lights.
The anti-collision light is controlled separately by `A/COL`. A fixed taxi
light and a fixed or swiveling landing light are separate exterior lights.
